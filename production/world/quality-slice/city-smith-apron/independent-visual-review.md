# Independent visual review — smith apron

Directly inspected all eight actual before/after PNGs using view_image on 2026-10-20 under assets/production/world-slice/city-smith-apron. Reviewed matched views 02_west_apron_approach, 03_workshop_overview, 04_east_entry and the limited 01_native_like_smith_wall. No render or geometry was changed by this review.

View 02 shows a material improvement over the empty apron: a teal-and-cream canopy, timber posts and braces frame a workbench with a tool rack, vise-like assembly and ingots. The stone pads visibly ground the foreground cluster. A separate rack, barrels and wheel form a secondary group at the far end. The workshop activity is communicated by recognizable objects rather than an added text label.

View 03 confirms these are two small clusters at opposite ends of the wall, with substantial empty central space retained. Their scale is modest relative to the large existing building; the canopy gives the western group a distinct silhouette while the eastern group stays lower. The existing building roof and wall still dominate the scene and remain visually simple. This package improves local dressing, not the whole district or architectural fidelity.

View 04 keeps the east entrance, threshold and interior anvil visible. Added storage sits to the left in this frame and does not visually cover the entrance. Parts of the new groups are cropped at the left edge, so this angle supports entrance readability rather than detailed asset inspection. Geometric clearance distances are established separately by the independent verifier, not inferred from this image.

View 01 is tightly framed on the existing blank wall and central reserved area. Almost none of the new props are visible apart from an edge fragment and changed shadow. It cannot demonstrate the package's visual quality or a meaningful improvement to that central wall view; this limitation is retained rather than treating all four camera pairs as equally informative.

Remaining limitations: the broad blank wall and uniform surrounding ground persist, the distant building context is still simple, and no character is present to establish normal gameplay composition. The existing dark gap/shadow along the building base remains visible; this additive prop package does not repair that building. Native appearance, avatar and companion movement, NPC interaction, and human art acceptance remain untested by these renders.

Decision: READY_FOR_NATIVE_ART_REVIEW for the bounded two-cluster smith-apron package. The inspected images support recognizable workshop dressing and clear entrance composition.
