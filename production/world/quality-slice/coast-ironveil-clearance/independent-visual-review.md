# Independent visual review: bounded coast clearance

Reviewer directly inspected all four PNGs with view_image: before/skyline.png, after/skyline.png, before/player_arrival.png and after/player_arrival.png under assets/production/world-slice/coast-ironveil-clearance. This is rendered evidence, not an inference from the geometry report.

The before skyline view is almost entirely covered by the green bank surface. In the after skyline view the upper gantry, stepped quarry terraces, ascent ramps, crystals, lower receiving yard and part of the weighhouse are visible. The foreground occlusion is materially resolved in this fixed view.

The before player-arrival view shows the weighhouse and cart under a dark overhead surface. The after view exposes the sky, tiled roof, timber framing, stone base, receiving gantry and background quarry rocks in daylight. The roof and house silhouette are readable, and the former dark ceiling no longer obscures the arrival composition. The stone base appears seated against the yard surface in this view.

Remaining concern: the after skyline view exposes long straight boundaries beneath the retained green banks, with a broad gray gap/open region behind the upper gantry. These cut edges read as an unfinished terrain termination rather than a natural retaining cliff. The geometry operation removes bank faces without constructing closed sidewalls; backface behavior and exposed boundaries therefore require native camera review. The surviving broad gray terraces and sparse upper gantry remain visibly blockout-like. These are not accepted as finished city art.

Decision: visual evidence supports the specific overhead-bank clearance repair. READY_FOR_NATIVE_CLEARANCE_RETEST, with open-surface/cut-edge art concern recorded. No full-city art, native Studio, collision, hero traversal, device or release acceptance is granted. The independent geometry report separately binds the repaired packet hash and verifies outside-mask preservation.
