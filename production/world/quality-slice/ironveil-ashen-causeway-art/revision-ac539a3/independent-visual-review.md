# Independent causeway visual review

Inspected all four actual before/after PNG pairs: Ironveil departure, Ashen arrival, corridor overview and underside within cut. Image, blend, source, camera, context and renderer hashes independently match their receipts; both recorded render exits are 0. The same existing coast cut and six-part road appear in both variants; the after variant adds only the reviewed causeway art packet. See independent-render-hashes.json.

The departure and arrival images now frame the route with repeated diagonal side trusses, making the deck edges much easier to read. The center walking view stays open toward the opposite settlement. The underside view shows transverse girders connected to side members, giving the former bare slab a visible support arrangement. The overview retains the landing-pad silhouettes and shows the two long side lines without adding a central pier into the void.

This is a bounded improvement, not final world art acceptance. The long span still looks very thin and repetitive; the deck remains a large blank surface and the surrounding quarry walls retain broad flat bands and sharp cuts. Foreground shore hides part of the south truss in the overview. Endpoint stone bearings are not clearly legible in these camera views, so their actual contact is established by the independent geometric test rather than visual assertion. The underside camera is an engineering inspection below the playable deck, not a normal player view. No obvious new shore surface cuts across the open center walking view in these images.

The trusses are noncolliding decoration and do not provide gameplay fall protection despite their railing appearance. Actors, dynamic resource art, GUI, private campaign pockets and distant city are omitted; Roblox materials, lighting, physics and gameplay are not reproduced. No native five-hero crossing, camera clipping, fall recovery, performance, multiplayer or human art acceptance is claimed.
