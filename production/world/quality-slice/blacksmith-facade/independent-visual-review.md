# Independent visual review — Blacksmith facade

Directly inspected all eight actual PNGs with view_image on 2026-10-20: the before/after pairs for 01_native_like_smith_wall, 02_west_apron_approach, 03_workshop_overview and 04_east_entry under assets/production/world-slice/blacksmith-facade. No Studio or Blender job was run by the reviewer. The shared v9 lease was valid immediately before this report was written.

View 01 shows a clear change to the previously blank wall: two pale upper panels, timber borders and diagonal braces, slatted vents and stone corner strips create readable architectural divisions. A visible masonry course fills the former continuous dark opening beneath much of the foundation edge. Existing workbench and stock clusters remain visible at the ends, and the ground-level central space remains visually open. This wave's view 01 includes the building elevation and both clusters; it provides useful facade evidence unlike the tightly cropped wall view in the earlier apron package.

View 02 confirms the upper-wall treatment reads from the west approach rather than only head-on. The existing canopy and tool rack remain legible in the foreground. Added stone and timber detailing ties the facade to those props, while the base course makes the wall appear more firmly seated. The large lower wall panels remain plain.

View 03 shows the treatment integrated with the existing building footprint and two apron clusters. The facade has more definition, but the broad roof surface and chimney remain simple, and the surrounding district still contains large uniform surfaces. This is a bounded facade improvement, not evidence that the entire building or city has reached final art quality.

View 04 shows added stone jambs, trim above the entrance and pale gable infill. The door opening and interior anvil remain visible. The existing foreground sign still obscures part of the left entrance edge, and the existing low rectangular sign near the threshold remains; those are not new facade obstructions. A residual dark recess is still visible near the corner/base in this oblique view, so the rendered evidence does not warrant claiming every foundation seam is visually eliminated.

Limitations: actors are omitted from all four pairs. These images cannot verify Borin's animation clearance, player scale, hero movement or actual NPC interaction. The separate source geometry and actor-bound reviews support only their stated mathematical checks. Lower walls, roof, chimney, surrounding ground and adjacent buildings remain visibly plain; the regular repeated vent/panel arrangement is a modest architectural treatment, not full environmental polish. Native material appearance, backfaces, clipping, depth conflicts and traversal still require actual Studio review.

Decision: READY_FOR_NATIVE_ART_REVIEW for the bounded Blacksmith facade packet. The images demonstrate a material improvement to the blank elevation while preserving visible entrance and apron composition. No full-city, final-polish, human UAT, native Studio or release acceptance is granted. Packet and source identity are recorded separately in independent-review.json and independent-actor-review.json.
