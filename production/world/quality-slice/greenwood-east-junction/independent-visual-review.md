# Independent visual review

Inspected all eight actual PNGs for arrival-east, road-return, junction-overview and landing-seam. Verified image/blend/input/source hashes and matching camera/context/renderer hashes; both recorded render exits are0. The renderer uses source-derived Greenwood pieces and portal posts/lintel plus existing local world-slice placements and old eastern road in both variants; only after adds the junction floor and stone facing. Its source bounds agree with the reviewed native Island/Landing/portal positions. Native collision-only support and Roblox behavior are not a rendered physics proof.

Before, the overview and two approach views show a clear missing strip between the arrival landing and eastern road. After, a continuous tan floor bridges that visual gap in both directions. The overview shows the junction meeting the existing road and arrival-side surfaces while preserving the portal structure. The seam view shows the new stone facing below the floor and avoids the previous exposed void immediately under the walking strip. Existing buildings, trees and portal remain visible.

The result is functional blockout improvement rather than finished environmental art. The new floor is broad and plain, its edge lacks a curb/rail, and the stone facing has large flat color blocks. The near landing kink and differing pavement materials remain visually abrupt. The existing portal still constrains the initial approach, and a screenshot cannot certify 22/24-stud width or the small vertical steps. Actors, dynamic resource art, GUI and native lighting/physics are omitted. No five-hero crossing, pathfinding, fall recovery, performance or human art acceptance is claimed.
