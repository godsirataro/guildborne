# Shared encounter participation — provisional contract

Pure server-side eligibility module only. No live registration, client remote, reward grant, Profile schema, settlement write or release flag is added. Thresholds are **PROVISIONAL before Public**, not a claim of balance or anti-abuse acceptance.

Factory: `SharedEncounterParticipation(identityPolicy, Now, {totalEnemyHP, encounterToken, activityWindow?, departureGrace?})`. The opaque encounter token comes from the server identity registry, not a parsed string or client field. The policy supplies `valid(a,b,ally)`, `key(actor)`, `encounter(actor)` and `ownerToken(actor)`. Every credited event requires active registered actors in that exact encounter. Player and NPC combat wrappers both use the registry's Hero kind and credit their human owner. No Party membership, proximity, mere join or kill-last-hit shortcut confers participation.

`Observe(seq,event,a,b,effectiveAmount)` accepts only damage_dealt or healing_done, a strictly increasing integer sequence from 1 through 2^31−1, and finite positive effective amount through 1e9. The caller must supply actual resolved server damage/healing, never attempted damage or client claims. Hero→Enemy damage credits the Hero owner. Enemy→Hero damage credits tanking to the recipient owner and records a wound against that exact actor-generation key. Hero→Hero healing credits at most outstanding enemy-caused wounds recorded for that recipient in this ledger; overheal, unrelated damage and repeated healing beyond that amount do not credit. Self-healing may consume a genuine recorded enemy wound but cannot create credit from full-health/overheal calls.

Admission bounds: 16 owners, 96 outstanding ally wound keys, no per-event history, per-category score saturated at 1e9. Wounds expire after the activity window (default 90 seconds) since the most recent enemy damage. Observe and Recover prune expired wounds and keys whose stored actor no longer resolves to the same active registration; removed generations cannot transfer wounds to a replacement. Capacity exhaustion fails closed and does not erase prior contribution.

Meaningful score is damage + effective healing + tanking (combined capped 1e9), compared with `max(1, ceil(fixed totalEnemyHP * .01))`. Fixed enemy HP must be finite in 1..1e9. Default recent activity window is 90 seconds and departure grace 30 seconds; options may shorten these windows. `Now` must remain finite, nonnegative and monotonic; invalid/reversed clocks fail closed. A dead or disconnected contributor retains earned credit within the applicable bounded windows without needing to remain attackable.

`Leave(ownerId)` freezes the first departure time and retires the observed owner registration token. Repeating Leave cannot extend grace. Departing without prior contribution adds no owner record. Effects from that retired registration are rejected. A legitimate new registration token may resume contribution while preserving earlier earned scores; only an effective newly accepted event clears departure and renews activity. Mere rejoin or attempted overheal does not renew eligibility. Registry removal must invalidate departed actors; this ledger does not own that lifecycle.

`Freeze()` returns `{owners, diagnostics, lastSeq, threshold}` once. Owners and per-owner diagnostics are sorted and bounded. Each return is a detached deep copy; subsequent events and Leave are rejected. A clock failure does not freeze the ledger. Root integration must join this eligibility snapshot with the trusted finite budget and immutable settlement preparation. Internal splitting of owner XP among player/NPCs remains a separate contract.

Verification: final token-aware focused runner passed **9 checks**, including solo, six plus outsider, NPC ownership, healer/tank credit, overheal, replay, invalid actor/amount, foreign encounter, late chip damage, inactivity, death, departure, old-registration rejection, legitimate rejoin, rejoin without action, immutable freeze, invalid clocks, owner bounds and 96-wound capacity. The first token-aware run failed while the additive identity methods were not yet present; it was rerun successfully after that dependency landed. Targeted static analysis passed for all three files and the module compiled. No Studio, real multi-client, cross-server or production evidence is claimed.

Review correction: each valid newer integer sequence is consumed **before** effect validation; rejected effects cannot be replayed later after the actor or wound state changes. `Recover(seq,actor,effectiveAmount)` consumes the same sequence stream and subtracts neutral/passive HP recovery from outstanding wounds without granting participation or renewing activity. The future shared runtime adapter must report **all** HP restoration through either healing_done or Recover exactly once; that integration is not yet present, so this module alone does not establish runtime anti-farming acceptance. Registered signed nonzero owner IDs support negative Studio clients consistently with identity; the sign never grants authority. Final focused tests now pass **11 checks**, including stale wounds at 91 seconds, detached-generation capacity reclamation, partial passive recovery, rejected sequence replay and negative owner IDs.
