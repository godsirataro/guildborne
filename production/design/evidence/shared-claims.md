# Shared PvE private durable recipient claims

Source implementation only; no cloud provider, world activation, public protocol, settlement delivery acknowledgement or shared reward rollout.

## Contract

`SharedPvEClaims.new(Content, Util, Economy)` provides `validateClaim`, `validateExtension`, `key`, `check(record, claim)` and `apply(record, command)`. The module also exposes static validators for ProfileSchema, so malformed optional receipts reject even when claim delivery is disabled. Existing constructors and schema versions remain unchanged. Old profiles may omit `data.sharedPvEClaims`; successful claims initialize `{version=1, receipts={}}` atomically.

Claim shape: `{namespace, encounterId, settlementVersion=2, xpPolicy="equal-actors-v1", allocation={ownerId,xp,gold,items,actorXP}}`. Full canonical allocation is stored under `namespace:encounterId`; namespace is at most24 and encounter ID at most64 ASCII identifier characters. There are at most32 durable receipts, with no eviction. Request IDs are separate bounded server attempt strings, not revision-derived or truncated claim identities. Before submission, at most33 candidates are checked against the32 recent operation IDs; this avoids collisions even when public commands deliberately pre-use those names. The chosen ID is then retained with its command revision through uncertainty. The counter is adapter-local and resets on recreation; it is not globally unique. Recent-operation collision checks and durable claim receipts, rather than counter uniqueness, provide correctness.

`PlayerDataService` accepts optional injected `SharedPvEClaims`; its private `CommitSharedPvE(userId, command)` validates recipient and full args, checks session state/closing/busy/lease, then durable receipts before revision-sensitive recent-result replay. The candidate checks again, and the fenced storage transform checks current durable receipts before comparing revision. Existing session/epoch ownership, audit bounds and pending-save recovery remain authoritative. Public Protocol rejects `CommitSharedPvE`.

`SharedPvERewardAdapter({Data,Repository,Claims,Util,Namespace}).Deliver(userId, encounterId)` reads the trusted `SharedPvESettlementStore` version2 repository, selects only the caller's allocation, and returns `Committed`/`Retry`/`Rejected` plus code. Repository injection must be the reviewed validating repository; a client-provided record is not accepted. Exact session object/token/epoch is captured before Read/Refresh and checked after each yield. Submitted command args and revision remain immutable while any PDS pending write exists. After an unknown returned exception, a later revision may replace that attempt only against the same active session with no busy/pending save, no durable claim and no matching committed operation; the original revision can no longer commit. Repository Read is followed by a fresh Active/busy/pending check before submission. Adapter recreation can resolve through Data.Refresh and the durable receipt; reconnect creates a fresh attempt and cannot duplicate a stored claim. A namespace must identify the same authority as the injected repository. Pending and in-flight maps each admit at most32 owners. Call Remove(userId, exactSession) on detach and Sweep() for departed/closing sessions; Deliver also sweeps. Cancellation of an adapter attempt never cancels or discards a PlayerDataService pending save. Per-attempt markers prevent stale yielded completions from clearing replacement work.

## Atomic grant and capacity

Frozen actorXP resolves saved owned character/adventurers, regardless of current party composition. Missing or unready actors and XP overflow reject the whole candidate. Existing ProfileLevelCap and LevelFromXP rules are retained. Gold uses Economy.gold and statistics.goldCreated; items use Economy.items. Neither market ledgers nor legacy CombatRewards/current party rewards are reused. Equipment instances use the reserved inventory.nextEquipmentSequence with short `shared:<sequence>:<index>` IDs.

Wallet, inventory, receipt count, audit or profile-size capacity leaves the entire allocation pending; other recipients can still claim their own allocations. There is no redistribution, clamping, partial grant, receipt eviction or guessed compensation. A conservative serialized-size upper bound models the full prospective command/audit envelopes before entering Saving. This may defer claims earlier than the physical byte limit; it does not promise32 maximum-sized claims fit every profile. Receipt-storage expansion or archival needs a separately reviewed migration.
