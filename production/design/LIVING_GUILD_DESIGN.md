# Living personal Guild
Status: PROPOSED UTILITY PROTOTYPE.

Retain Hall's approved level-cap formula and current Blacksmith/crafting transactions. Prioritize a Training Ground and Strategy Table that help players understand existing skills and prepare their owned team. Hero Quarters/rest, Warehouse/materials and trophies must reuse their existing ownership/capacity boundaries.

First prototype: a safe practice space with no economic drops or persistent combat rewards; try current orders and formation, reset only explicitly isolated practice actors. Strategy Table initially opens reviewed team/skill preparation views; do not advertise saved tactical presets until versioned storage exists. A UI shortcut alone is not a completed building utility.

Design practice admission, exit, death/reset, item ownership and cleanup before runtime changes. Visitors and human Party leaders cannot edit the owner's build or heroes. Practice encounters cannot advance ordinary quests or duplicate combat receipts. Research Room and three proposed specialization branches belong to GL-2.0 P5 before Public; exact node IDs/costs await the P0 named inventory.

Acceptance: failure in practice returns safely; no XP/gold/items minted; preparation produces visible behavior in a subsequent real encounter; ordinary placement permissions/costs remain valid; mobile navigation and human usefulness tested. New building IDs/costs require an explicit catalog decision, not invented placeholder assets.
