# Game design pillars
Status: APPROVED DIRECTION / IMPLEMENTATION VARIES.

Lead Your Heroes. Build Your Guild. Fight Together.

Guildborne combines player-controlled action, five owned NPC companions, personal Guild preparation and optional human cooperation. The core loop is prepare/manage → explore/fight → earn/progress → upgrade Guild → prepare again. Solo progression remains viable. Human Parties contain at most six owners; Federation is a permanent association of owners required before first Public, never additional human members inside a personal Guild.

A 10–20 minute useful session and early tactical discovery are design targets, not measured results. Preserve Lv10/35/70 class gates, Hall caps and earned legacy progression. Public requires GL-2.0 Lv1–100/full campaign; Lv35/two arcs remains an internal milestone. Tactical choices should change positioning and timing before adding damage multipliers. Visuals and sounds explain server-decided outcomes.

Public scope remains LAUNCH_PLAN.md. Expansion priority is not release availability. No retention, popularity or competitiveness guarantee. Existing modules and scoped source evidence take precedence over claims in the archived proposal.
