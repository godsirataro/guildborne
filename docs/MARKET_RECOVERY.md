# Market recovery and reconciliation

The existing profile-owner bridge remains authoritative. Workers may inspect foreign committed source provenance but cannot modify another server's profile. Profile reservation, accepted-book deposit, source outcome and claim acknowledgment remain separate recoverable commits. No timeout is proof of an aborted transfer.

Recovery is idempotent across callback reruns, competing workers, missing notifications and index loss. It rotates five item keys, checks up to two CREATED tickets with a rotating cursor, processes expiry/lifecycle, and applies up to twelve claims per online owner. Profile claim credit plus its cumulative item watermark is atomic; the later book acknowledgment may retry. Capacity failures stop the ordered claim prefix and leave durable pending state. Audit export drains after market requests as well as maintenance so the existing bounded profile audit journal does not become an artificial load bottleneck.

At closure, CREATED becomes permanently ABORTED and remaining escrow becomes claims. Accepted sources must be confirmed after their owner committed the source outcome. Reconciliation refuses unresolved sources/claims. After archive, EXPORTED/CLOSED/REFUNDED profile transfers may compact through archivedThrough; PREPARED sources must resolve before compaction. A stale archived reservation cannot become a new-generation order.

`AuditEpoch` is a read-only single-book invariant audit: order original=filled+remaining (terminal remaining is historical cancelled quantity, not spendable escrow), exact active escrow, receipt/order correspondence, claim sequence uniqueness, gross=net+fee, cumulative seller fee, and total input escrow = remaining escrow + all output claims + burned fees. An audit failure preserves state and blocks unsafe transformation. The adapter latches PAUSED on a MarketInvariant error; it does not mint a repair.

`MarketReconciliation` additionally audits a supplied **quiescent closed cohort** of profile snapshots against starting Gold/items plus explicit legitimate outside adjustments. It counts accepted bridge assets once, unapplied claims by profile watermark, pending source reservations and archived/current fees. It rejects missing participants. Tests invoke it after every load cycle. It is not a globally atomic live supply estimate: collect consistent snapshots while the test cohort is idle; a racing snapshot must be retried, not treated as permission to compensate.

READ_ONLY_AUDIT never mutates. RECOVERY_ASSIST invokes only ordinary replay/rebuild/expiry/lifecycle transitions. No operator function grants Gold/items, alters prices, erases escrow or force-acknowledges an uncredited claim. [Operations](MARKET_OPERATIONS_RUNBOOK.md) covers incidents.
