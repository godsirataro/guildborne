# Market epochs — Phase 4.5

Each item has an independent, cloud-controlled integer generation. The fixed authority key remains `GB_Market_<environment>/b:<item>:1`; the trailing 1 is the stable storage slot, not the current generation. Schema-v2 book fields `epoch`, `archivedThrough`, `phase` and monotonic order/trade/claim counters fence all transitions in that one UpdateAsync. No client clock, transient lease, routing-key transaction or new key per epoch is needed.

`ACTIVE → CLOSING → RECONCILED → ARCHIVED → ACTIVE(next generation)` is the controlled lifecycle. Closing stops new admission, aborts unfunded tickets, cancels every open order and creates refund claims. No open order carries forward. Existing accepted sources must be durably confirmed and all claims acknowledged before reconciliation succeeds. Archive also requires the configured 300-second minimum replay window after close. Pending/offline recipients intentionally keep this item CLOSING; other items continue. There is no automatic confiscation, expiry of claims, or promise to finish while recipients remain absent or their capacity is full.

Recovery performs one lifecycle step per visit. An ACTIVE item closes at the configured one-day epoch age or 128-ticket cap. Operators may initiate closure earlier. With five items visited every 30 seconds, each item is visited approximately every 150 seconds; lifecycle phases can take several visits. It is an availability bound, not a transaction deadline. Operators can inspect and advance the same validated transitions. Missing messages or MemoryStore leases cannot bypass any state.

`StartEpoch` atomically advances the generation/fence, clears reconciled raw orders/receipts/claims/trades and keeps cumulative sequences, aggregate history and fee totals. Stale commands include their expected generation and are rejected even after all individual receipts are gone. The UI submits its displayed generation; a stale confirmation requests refresh. A v1 worker rejects schema v2 rather than rewriting it. Drain old binaries before deployment; a suspended old worker may require reconnect on the new reader to recover its reserved profile asset.

Sequence priority is per item, serialized by accepted book updates. It is not wall-clock request order across servers. Both sides stay in one authority key to retain atomic escrow transfer; independent buy/sell authority shards would reintroduce the multi-key transaction problem. [Archival](MARKET_ARCHIVAL.md) and [recovery](MARKET_RECOVERY.md) define the replay guarantees.
