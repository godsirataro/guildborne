## Explicit scope and nonclaims

- **410 city inhabitants proposed in the source roster**: Central City 80 + six civic cities x55. This is not 410 physical NPCs spawned simultaneously and is not a tested capacity limit. Other areas will receive additional design budgets after actual zone/profiler evidence.
- **90 reusable role/archetype proposals**: not 90 new implemented NPCs, nor 90 active scripts.
- **13 existing body IDs, 4 cosmetic races**: Catalog planned definitions, not a claim of 13 finished imported rigs/cages. Any 17–21 true-body goal requires separate visual/perf review and owner signoff.
- **16 optional secret concepts**: none is silently a shipped canonical quest. Existing main-story hidden routes remain MAIN.
- **294 proposed QA cases**: every one is `NOT_RUN`, with no fabricated test result. Current build should be re-tested only in an authorized isolated environment.
- No files are pushed to GitHub, no Roblox asset uploaded, no DataStore written, no paid service used and no other chat session commanded merely by generating this package.
