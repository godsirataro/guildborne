# Crown Road cast

Four original simplified character models: human courier, dwarf traveler, elf traveler and crossroads keeper. Concept: `../generated/guildborne-crown-road-cast-v1.png`; exact built-in image prompt: `../CROWN_ROAD_CAST_PROMPT.md`.

The shared kit has 124 visible box parts / 1,488 triangles, four 16-bone rigs, 16 baked clips and 24 FBX/GLB exports. Every export passed rig/weights/triangles checks; animated exports also passed sampled motion, stationary root and loop seam checks. Individual `.blend` files retain editable rigs/actions. Gallery scales characters for comparison; individual models retain their authored proportions.

`NoviceCharacterKit.luau` uses the same geometry with native Motor6D articulation. Courier, dwarf and elf bind to the optional course. Keeper replaces the training sentinel in the Chapter 4 lesson. Native appearance is a simplified interpretation of the concept, not a pixel-identical reproduction. No cloud mesh or animation IDs are assigned.

Actual courier E interaction advanced the objective and animated four joints. A delayed-joint/tag removal/re-add/stream-out fixture passed. Keeper range feedback displayed Thai without damage at 20 studs; real input won its temporary-eligibility fixture at 48/48 HP. Subsequent custom-joint mapping yielded nine animated joints during a real warning; leave removed arena/HUD. These are local technical checks, not human/device approval or a new complete four-character journey.
