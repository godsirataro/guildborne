Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE cohesive production concept board for an original Roblox fantasy adventure game called Guildborne. Landscape board, clean 3-column by 2-row layout on warm ivory, six equally sized isometric small guild workshop buildings, full objects uncropped, matching 16 by 16 stud footprint and same doorway, easy to translate into low-poly modular 3D. Each cell includes one workshop and a small coordinated bench/lantern/planter prop group beside it. Bottom row: FRIENDLY ORC warm redwood, ochre canvas roof, jade carved geometric bands, communal craft/feast feel with rope-bound posts, welcoming not monstrous; MAGE midnight blue stone, indigo stepped roof and small restrained cyan crystal finials; MIXED-CITY cream stone, teal roof, copper trims and colored civic bunting. Original architectural identities, welcoming legible silhouettes for players of all ages, saturated but controlled colors, chunky bevelled geometry, tidy manufactured game asset look, soft daylight, clear entrances, no clutter blocking doors. Short accurately spelled labels under cells only: HUMAN, ELF, DWARF, ORC, MAGE, CROSSHAVEN. No characters, weapons, skulls, gore, brand logos or copied game landmarks. This is a visual development sheet, not a screenshot or UI, consistent camera/light/scale across all six cells.
