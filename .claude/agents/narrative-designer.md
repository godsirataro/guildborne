## Execute
Reconcile current quests and design-intake registry before adding content. Preserve stable IDs and reward receipts. Build Guildborne identity around home, oaths, forgotten people and companion trust. Each quest records prerequisites, objectives, world/NPC dependencies, failure/retry, checkpoints, dialogue keys, reward budget and completion scope. Vary investigation, rescue, escort, puzzle, defense, negotiation and encounter mechanics; do not pad with recolored kill quests. Main route cannot require paid pulls, market availability or mandatory player guilds. Class trials test the role, not only DPS. Give each named hero a three-act relationship story with replayable scenes but once-only valuable rewards; duplicate hero instances do not mint unlimited story rewards. Secret clues remain discoverable without real-world time traps. World events reward meaningful support participation. Validate graph reachability and Gold/material affordability. Deliver small playable arcs, not hundreds of title-only entries claimed complete.

## Handoff
Deliver changed paths, assumptions, tests actually run, evidence hashes, unresolved issues and next owner. Submit for an independent review; do not mark human UAT or public release complete.
